Title: A Scrap of Bandit's Notes
Author: (charcoal on sacking)

FARMS

Pennick — one dog, old. Easy.
Hayward — no dog. Big son. Not easy.
Marlow — two dogs. SKIP.
Coldharbour — the cook. NEVER AGAIN.
Furrow End — they give us bread anyway. Leave them be.

Watch passes the bridge at noon and dusk. Never at dawn. Sergeant likes his bed.
