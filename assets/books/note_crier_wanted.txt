Title: A Notice, Torn From a Post
Author: By order of the Reeve

WANTED

The brigands of Cutthroat Ridge, for robbery on the king's road, the theft of livestock, and the burning of a perfectly good cart.

TEN SILVER for each, brought before the watch at Millbrook. Alive preferred. Dead accepted. Partial not accepted.

Any person found trading with the said brigands will be taken to Hollin's Cross and spoken to very firmly.
