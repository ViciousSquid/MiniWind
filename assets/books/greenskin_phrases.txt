Title: A Few Useful Words of the Greenskin Tongue
Author: Tamsin Hollow, trader, formerly of the Snaggletooth camps

For the traveller who finds himself among the greenskins and would like to leave with all his fingers. I have lived among them two summers. Their speech is harder than it sounds, and it sounds very hard.

A greenskin does not respect a man who is afraid. He does not respect a man who is not afraid, either; he thinks that man is stupid. What he respects is a man who is afraid and talks anyway. Talk, then. Keep talking. Talking is how you tell a greenskin you are worth more alive.

Some words:

"Grak." Hello. Also goodbye. Also, in the right tone, "I am about to hit you." Practise the tone.

"Grak-grak." Hello, friend. Only to be used once you have shared food. If you use it before, it is very funny to them, and they will laugh, and then they will hit you.

"Nub." No. Say it firmly. A greenskin who hears a weak no hears a yes.

"Mog skraa." This is a good trade. Say it whether or not it is. If it is a bad trade, say it with a sigh, and they may give you a little more, out of pity, which they do have, though they hide it.

"Shak ur'bok." Your axe is very large. This is the highest compliment you can pay a greenskin warrior and it works on the women as well as the men. It also works, I am told, on blacksmiths.

"Ur nub grak." I am not here. This is what you say when you have walked into the wrong tent. It does not work, but they appreciate the effort.

Finally: never, ever, under any circumstances, eat the soup.
