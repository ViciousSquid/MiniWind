Title: The Ash That Speaks
Author: (no name; the cover is scorched)

A tract of the Ashen Circle. Bound in grey cloth, smelling of smoke. The hand is careful and very small.

The fire went out on the Emberpeaks, they tell you. The firekeepers let it die, and came down the mountain, and became farmers and smiths, and forgot.

We did not forget.

The fire did not go out. It went down. It went into the mountain, and under the mountain, and it waits there, in the red dark, and it speaks to those who will listen. It speaks in the language of ash.

When the Emberpeaks smoke, that is the fire breathing. When the ash falls grey on Oakridge, that is the fire writing. Gather it. Read it. It says: the Vale was the fire's before it was the Seven's, and it will be the fire's after.

The first rite: sit with a burning coal in your palm until it stops hurting. It will stop hurting. Everything stops hurting, in the end, if you hold it long enough.

The second rite is not written. It is given, in the Circle, by those who have earned it.

The third rite is the last rite, and few are chosen for it, and those who are chosen do not return to tell of it, for they have gone into the fire and become the fire's voice.

Do not fear the dark under the hills. The dark is only the place where the fire has not yet reached.

Do not fear the Watch. The Watch is only men.

Do not fear the dragon. The dragon is the fire's own child, and it knows its kin.

(Somebody has written underneath, in a shaking hand: IT DID NOT KNOW HIM.)
