Title: A List, in Small Neat Writing
Author: (ash smeared along the edge)

To be given to the fire, when the mountain speaks:

The reeve, who keeps the stores and gives nothing.
The priest at Saint Aldric's, who preaches against the flame.
The smith at Millbrook, who would not forge the circle.
Bryn of the High Fold, who saw us on the hill. (Done.)
The pedlar Dunstan, who talks too much.

And any who come asking after these names.
