Title: The Millbrook Crier — Torn Page
Author: The Millbrook Crier

WOLVES TAKE SIX SHEEP AT HIGH FOLD

Bryn of the High Fold reports the loss of six ewes to the Greywood pack in a single night, the worst toll in living memory. "It was the old grey bitch," said Bryn. "She knows the dogs' rounds better than the dogs do."

The reeve has promised a bounty of two silver a pelt. Hunters are reminded that the bounty does not apply to dogs, foxes, or "a sort of large cat", as was attempted last spring.
