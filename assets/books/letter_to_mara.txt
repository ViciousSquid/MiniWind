Title: A Letter, Much Folded
Author: Kestrel

Mara —

I am writing this because every time I try to say it to your face your brother comes round the corner with a pitchfork, and I am beginning to think he does it on purpose.

The harvest fair is in ten days. There will be a fiddler from Ashby, and cider, and the reeve has promised a roast pig. I would very much like it if you would walk there with me. If you would rather not, tear this up and say nothing and I will never mention it again, and we will go on as we were, and I will be content with that, mostly.

If you would, put a sprig of heather in your window, and I will know.

[page]

I have been past your house four times today. There is no heather in the window yet. I am trying to be patient.

There is still no heather. Your brother saw me this time.

There is heather.

— K.
