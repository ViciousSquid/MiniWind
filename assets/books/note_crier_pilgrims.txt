Title: The Millbrook Crier — Clipping
Author: The Millbrook Crier

PILGRIM SEASON OPENS AT THE MOONWELL

The white cairns have been rebuilt after the winter, and Sister Ilse of the Pilgrim's Lantern reports beds for twenty. Pilgrims are asked to add a stone to each cairn and to stop taking them home as keepsakes, as last year the third cairn vanished altogether.
