Title: Corbin's Ledger
Author: Hal Corbin

Mill tolls, the Cross. Took the strongbox the day we came. Kept it shut. A
locked box is worth more to a reeve than an open one.

Wheat in: none. Flour out: eleven sacks, all we found in the loft. "To the
boat." Wat says the boat is late. Wat says a lot of things.

Silver, for mill-work, paid at the south shore: thirty. Silver, for keeping
the Cross's runner off this road: twelve. Paid the runner off instead. Cheaper.

The miller's sails. Do NOT let the lads turn them. Smoke and sails both carry
for miles, and the Reeve has eyes.

Ned's share: owed. Jory's share: owed. Wat's share: Wat knows what he did.

The Ash-men's price, settled.

Rook wants to know when I'm coming home. Told him when the boat comes.
