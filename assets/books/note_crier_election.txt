Title: The Millbrook Crier — Clipping
Author: The Millbrook Crier

REEVE RETURNED UNOPPOSED (AGAIN)

Aldric was this week returned as Reeve of the Vale for a third term of seven years, no other candidate having come forward. Asked why, the reeve said that the people had spoken. Asked what they had said, the reeve said that they had said nothing, and that this was very much the point.

A petition calling for "anyone else" gathered forty names before the paper ran out.
