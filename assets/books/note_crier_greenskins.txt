Title: The Millbrook Crier — Clipping
Author: The Millbrook Crier

GREENSKINS SIGHTED NEAR THE WARCAMP

Travellers on the north road report greenskin war-parties beyond the old watchtower. The watch advises that the road is "mostly safe in daylight".

Asked what "mostly" meant, a guardsman said that it meant what it said. Asked about the night, he went back inside.
