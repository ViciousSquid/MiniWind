Title: Heron
Author: (a poem, copied out neatly)

Grey on grey at the water's edge,
she stands as still as the standing reeds,
and the lake forgets that she is there,
and the fish forget, and so do I —

till the long neck strikes like a dropped stone,
and the water closes, and she is gone,
and the evening is just as still as before,
but one thing less, and one thing more.
