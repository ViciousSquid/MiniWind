Title: A Ledger, Water-Stained
Author: Kept by someone at Brigand's Hollow who could count

Accounts, such as they are. The ink has run in places.

Took from the Kettlewick road: 2 sacks barley, 1 pig (escaped), 1 cartwheel (why), 14 coppers, 1 silver ring (brass).

Took from the pedlar on the Brackenford bridge: 3 knives, 1 good cloak, 1 bad poem (he insisted), 8 coppers. Pedlar says he will remember us. Pedlar can remember us from the bottom of the river.

[page]

Owed to Mags for mending the crossbow: 6 coppers. Paid: 0. Mags says the crossbow will break again if not paid. Mags is very probably right.

Owed to the Ridge for passage: one tenth of all. Paid: one twentieth. Do not tell the Ridge.

Spent at the Wanderer's Rest: 22 coppers. Spent on what: unclear. Ask Fen. Fen does not remember. Nobody remembers. There is a goat now.

[page]

Shares:

Captain — four parts.
Fen — two parts (one part, since the goat).
Wil — two parts.
The quiet one — one part, and he has not complained, which worries me.
Me — two parts, for the counting, which nobody else can do, which is why I am writing this down, so that when somebody cuts my throat for the money they will at least know how much there was.

[page]

Last entry, in a hurried hand:

Watch came up the hollow at dawn. Twenty of them. Captain says we hold. I say we run. If you are reading this, I expect you know which of us was right.
