Title: The Journal of Aldous Penn
Author: Aldous Penn, adventurer (so he says)

Day the first. Left the Wanderer's Rest at dawn with a sword, a sack, a lantern, and a map I paid two silver for from a man who swore it showed a dragon's hoard. I do not believe in dragons. I do believe in hoards.

Day the second. The map is wrong. Not a little wrong. It shows a river where there is a hill, and a hill where there is a different, larger hill. Followed it anyway. Found a cave. The map does not show the cave, which I choose to take as a good sign.

Day the third. Inside. Cold, damp, dark, as expected. Lantern holding. Found a chest, already opened, already empty. Somebody was here before me. Found the somebody, too, a little further in. He was not in a talkative mood. Took his boots; mine leak.

Day the fourth. Something is following me. I hear it when I stop walking. When I turn around there is nothing. When I walk again, it walks again. I am telling myself it is the echo. I am not a fool. It is not the echo.

Day the fifth. Found the hoard. It is not a hoard. It is a heap of bones, and on top of the bones, a few coins, a dented helm, and somebody's journal, rather like this one, which ends rather like I fear this one will.

I have read it. He also bought the map from the man at the Wanderer's Rest.
