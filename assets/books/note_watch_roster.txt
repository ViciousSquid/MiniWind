Title: Watch Roster, Pinned to a Door
Author: Sergeant of the Watch

North gate, dawn: Roderick
North gate, dusk: Roderick (AGAIN — Roderick, this is because of the cheese)
Bridge, noon: Kestrel
Bridge, dusk: Kestrel and whoever is sober
Hollin's Cross: Tam (until he comes back)
Night round: everybody, in turns, no swapping for ale, I KNOW about the ale

Anyone found asleep at their post will do the night round for a month.
