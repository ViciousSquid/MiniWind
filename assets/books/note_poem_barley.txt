Title: Barley
Author: (scrawled on a flour sack, by someone at Oakridge)

Barley in the morning, barley in the rain,
barley in my dinner and barley in my brain,
I cut it and I bind it and I stack it in the barn,
and when I die they'll bury me in barley, I'll be bound.

(It doesn't rhyme at the end. I KNOW. Leave it.)
