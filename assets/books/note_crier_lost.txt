Title: The Millbrook Crier — Lost & Found
Author: The Millbrook Crier

LOST: one brown nanny goat, answers to Duchess, last seen on the Kettlewick road in the company of three men of poor character. Reward for her return. The men may keep themselves.

FOUND: one left boot, good leather, at the edge of Greywood. Owner may collect from the watch-house. The watch would also like a word with the owner, as the boot was not empty.
