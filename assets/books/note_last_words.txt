Title: A Note, Folded Small
Author: (in a shaking hand)

If anyone finds this, I am Harl of Brackenford.

Tell my wife Edda the money is under the third board from the hearth. Tell her I did not go for the gold, whatever they say. I went because of the boy, and I found him, and he is past help, and so am I now.

Tell her to sell the cart. Tell her I am sorry about the cart.
