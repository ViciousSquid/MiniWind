Title: The Millbrook Crier — Clipping
Author: The Millbrook Crier

SMOKE ON THE EMBERPEAKS: "NOTHING TO WORRY ABOUT"

Smoke was seen rising from the Emberpeaks for the fourth day running, and grey ash fell on the barley at Oakridge. The reeve's office says the mountains "have always smoked" and there is "nothing to worry about".

The Crier notes that the reeve's office has moved its strongbox to the cellar.
